# Product Vision

## Purpose

MKE Service Equity is a civic decision-support platform that helps Milwaukee public-sector decision-makers understand where public-service needs, structural inequities, existing resources, and government response intersect geographically.

The first production module is the **MKE Food Equity Atlas**.

## Problem

Milwaukee has substantial demographic, health, service, infrastructure, and public-investment data, but decision-makers often encounter these data in separate systems and maps. A planner may know where poverty is concentrated, where grocery stores are located, or where public investments are occurring, but not see those conditions together in one transparent analytical workflow.

The result is a recurring planning problem:

> Where is need concentrated, what services already exist, and where does the evidence suggest further investigation is warranted?

## Product thesis

The Atlas should not merely show where inequity exists. It should help decision-makers understand where service availability appears misaligned with measured need.

## Primary users

- City and County planners
- Strategy staff
- Program managers
- Department leaders
- Public-health staff
- Policy staff
- Elected-office staff
- Senior leadership

## Secondary users

- Community organizations
- Researchers
- Journalists
- Advocates
- Residents

## Geographic model

- Coverage: Milwaukee County
- Initial analytical focus: City of Milwaukee
- Canonical analytical geography: 2020 Census tract
- Supporting geographies: ZIP code, municipality, neighborhood, aldermanic district, supervisory district, and service boundaries where reliable

## Product principles

1. **Build narrow. Architect broad.**
2. **Evidence before recommendation.**
3. **Transparent and reproducible scoring.**
4. **Need and response remain analytically separate.**
5. **The map is not the only way to access evidence.**
6. **Mobile is a first-class experience.**
7. **AI may explain verified evidence later; AI never creates official scores.**
8. **Describe neighborhood conditions without labeling neighborhoods or residents as deficient.**

## Long-term platform

The shared platform may eventually support:

- Food Equity
- Housing Equity
- Health Equity
- Transit Equity
- Parks Equity
- Infrastructure Equity
- Public Investment analysis

The MVP builds only Food Equity.
