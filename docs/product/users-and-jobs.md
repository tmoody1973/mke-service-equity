# Users and Jobs

## Primary user group

Milwaukee public-sector planners, strategy staff, program managers, department leaders, policy staff, and elected-office staff.

### Their needs

- locate concentrations of need
- explain why an area is flagged
- compare areas consistently
- distinguish need from existing response
- identify potential service gaps
- cite defensible evidence in meetings and planning documents

## Secondary users

### Community organizations
Need understandable evidence for advocacy, program design, and partnership conversations.

### Journalists and researchers
Need transparent sources, definitions, tract comparisons, and downloadable evidence.

### Residents
Need a clear view of local conditions and existing resources without GIS expertise.

## UX consequence

The application is built first as a professional decision-support tool but must remain understandable and usable by the public.
